Breach Point: Warehouse

Clear a procedurally built warehouse. Every hostile carries a layered body: clothing, skin in three layers (epidermis, dermis, fat), muscle, skeleton, organs and major vessels. Rounds strip each layer they pass through and lose energy as they go.
